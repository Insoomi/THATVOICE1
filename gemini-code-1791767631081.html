Who We Are

THATVOICE is a youth-centred advocacy and leadership initiative committed to building a generation of informed, confident and responsible young people in Northern Uganda and beyond. Through advocacy, public speaking, debate, human rights education and leadership development, we create opportunities for young people to express themselves, challenge injustice constructively and participate in shaping their communities.
