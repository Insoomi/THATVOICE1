About THATVOICE

Empowering young people to speak confidently and shape their future.

Who We Are & Our Story

THATVOICE is a youth-centred advocacy and leadership platform founded to address the gaps in youth representation, public speaking confidence, and civic engagement. Operating primarily from Northern Uganda, our initiative bridges the gap between youthful energy and constructive community development.

[Founder / History Placeholder: Founded by Innocent Omoo Lubangakene, THATVOICE grew out of a passionate belief that when young people are given platforms, training, and encouragement, they become powerful catalysts for positive social change.]

Our Vision

A society where every young person is empowered to speak confidently, exercise their rights and participate meaningfully in shaping their future.

Our Mission

To empower young people through advocacy, human rights education, debate, public speaking, leadership development and community engagement.

Our Core Values

Voice

Everyone deserves a meaningful opportunity to be heard.

Courage

Speak up for what is right and challenge injustice constructively.

Integrity

Act honestly, ethically, and responsibly in all endeavours.

Equality

Respect the dignity and rights of every person equally.

Inclusion

Create opportunities for diverse and underrepresented voices.

Leadership

Inspire service, accountability, and positive community change.

Safeguarding & Inclusion

THATVOICE is strictly committed to creating safe, respectful, and inclusive environments for all participants, volunteers, and partners. We uphold zero-tolerance policies regarding harassment, discrimination, or exploitation, ensuring that youth participation is safe, ethical, and empowering.
