Our Programmes

Building skills, fostering dialogue, and championing youth rights.

A. Advocacy & Human Rights

Overview: Promotes fundamental human rights awareness, equality, access to justice, social accountability, and constructive advocacy among youth.

Target Participants: Students, youth groups, and community advocates.

Expected Outcomes: Increased civic literacy and empowered young defenders of community rights.

B. Debate & Public Speaking

Overview: Trains students and young people in rigorous research, structured argumentation, critical thinking, and respectful public discourse.

Target Participants: Secondary school students and university youth.

Expected Outcomes: Articulate, confident young speakers capable of constructive public debate.

C. Youth Leadership & Mentorship

Overview: Supports emerging leaders through ethical leadership training, peer mentorship, teamwork, and service-oriented responsibility.

Target Participants: Young student leaders and aspiring community organizers.

Expected Outcomes: Accountable, ethical, and community-focused young leaders.

D. Civic Education & Participation

Overview: Encourages informed participation in community discussions, local governance, and public affairs.

Target Participants: Out-of-school youth and community members.

Expected Outcomes: Active grassroots participation in local development processes.

E. Youth Journalism & Creative Expression

Overview: Provides platforms for youth to communicate community issues through poetry, storytelling, journalism, and creative writing.

Target Participants: Young writers, poets, and aspiring student journalists.

Expected Outcomes: Amplified grassroots stories communicated through ethical media.

F. Community Engagement

Overview: Facilitates community dialogues, youth-led campaigns, and locally relevant social-change projects.

Target Participants: Schools, local communities, and civil society partners.

Expected Outcomes: Sustainable grassroots solutions developed and owned by young people.
